About Us

A calmer, data-aware place to understand your day.

Welcome to Lumevine, a personal wellness workspace built to help you understand what may support you today, notice patterns over time, and shape routines that fit your real life.

We believe wellness is not about being perfect or filling every tracker. It is about using the information you choose to record, recognizing tentative patterns, and making small, thoughtful changes over time.

1. Who We Are

Lumevine brings everyday wellness activities into one place. Today’s Focus uses available mood, sleep, hydration, and task information to suggest a relevant next step instead of showing every feature with equal weight.

Our goal is to provide a thoughtful digital space where you can check in, organize a real morning-to-night routine, reflect on your week, and develop a clearer understanding of your day-to-day well-being.

2. Our Mission

Our mission is to make personal wellness feel more approachable, understandable, and manageable. We want to encourage people to pay attention to their habits and take meaningful steps toward a lifestyle that works for them.

We believe that progress can begin with something small: a moment of reflection, a healthier routine, or simply taking the time to understand how your day is going.

3. What Lumevine Offers

Lumevine brings together features intended to support personal organization, self-reflection, and everyday wellness.

  • Today’s Focus: See one primary check-in, a compact read of today’s signals, and adaptive priorities based on what you have actually logged.
  • Daily Routine: Generate a saved morning-to-night routine, add or replace activities, choose time ranges, and keep the structure flexible.
  • Wellness tracking: Record personal mood, sleep, hydration, tasks, gratitude, and other check-ins without requiring a perfect streak.
  • Weekly reflection: Review tentative connections between mood, sleep, routines, hydration, music, and task completion when enough data is available.
  • Habit Journey: Earn progress through quests and recovery actions, with supportive streak protection rather than pressure to be perfect.
  • AI Companion: Talk through everyday thoughts, boredom, routines, goals, and progress with a persistent Firebase-backed profile of useful goals, preferences, challenges, interests, and progress.
  • Personal controls: Choose a theme, manage reminders, export or import account data, and clear stored information when you want to start fresh.

4. Our Philosophy

Awareness: Understanding your habits is a useful starting point for making intentional choices.

Balance: Wellness should complement your life, not become another source of pressure.

Consistency: Small, realistic actions can help you build routines that are easier to maintain.

Self-kindness: Everyone's circumstances are different. We believe personal progress deserves patience rather than perfectionism.

5. Your Privacy Matters

Personal wellness information can be sensitive. We encourage you to understand how information is handled when using Lumevine and to avoid entering information you would not want stored with your account.

For details about information collection, storage, use, and your available choices, please read our Privacy Policy .

6. A Note About Wellness

Lumevine is intended to support general well-being and self-reflection. App summaries, wellness scores, forecasts, and AI-generated responses may be incomplete or inaccurate. They are not medical assessments, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations and should not be used to make medical decisions.

For health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
