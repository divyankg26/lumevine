1. Getting Started with Lumevine

Understand the app, its purpose, and how its features fit together.

What is Lumevine actually designed to help me do?

Lumevine brings together wellness tracking, routines, reflection, personal organization, and AI-assisted conversations. Rather than relying on one number to describe your well-being, you can record different parts of your daily life and review them over time.

You can use the features that are useful to you without needing to complete every tracker or maintain a perfect routine.

Which features should I set up first?

Start with the parts of Lumevine you want to use consistently. For example, you might begin with mood check-ins, sleep tracking, hydration, or a daily routine.

Record information as you go, then explore the relevant summaries and reflections. If a feature depends on historical entries, it may not provide a meaningful trend immediately.

Do I need to use every feature for Lumevine to be useful?

No. Track only the information that is relevant to you. Some summaries may have less information available when you leave certain trackers unused, but you should not feel pressured to record everything every day.

Is Lumevine a medical or mental health app?

Lumevine is a wellness and self-reflection tool, not a substitute for medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or professional mental health care. Scores, forecasts, and AI responses do not establish whether you have a health condition.

If you have persistent or concerning symptoms, consult a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on an app-generated result.

Can I use Lumevine without following a perfect routine?

Absolutely. Missed days happen. A missed check-in is not a personal failure, and a tracking streak is not a measure of your worth. Use your records to learn what works for you, not to punish yourself for being inconsistent.

Why is a feature or section missing from my app?

First, make sure you are using the intended Lumevine website or application and that the page has finished loading. Some features may depend on your current app version, sign-in state, configuration, or whether the feature has been enabled.

  1. Refresh the app and navigate back to the feature.
  2. Check whether you are signed in to the intended account.
  3. Look for an error message or a disabled control.
  4. If the feature is still missing, contact support with its name and a description of what you see.

Do not assume that a missing section means your account data has been deleted.

2. Tracking, Wellness Scores & Daily Check-ins

Questions about recorded information, changing scores, and missing data.

Why is my wellness score zero, blank, or unavailable?

A zero, blank value, or unavailable result does not necessarily mean that your well-being is poor. The result may depend on the information available to the relevant feature, whether required entries have been recorded, or whether a calculation has completed.

  1. Check whether the relevant mood, sleep, hydration, or other requested records have been saved.
  2. Look for missing entries or incomplete check-ins.
  3. Refresh the page and allow the feature to load.
  4. If the value remains unexpected, report the score name, approximate time, and steps that led to it.

The exact meaning of zero or blank can differ by feature. Do not interpret it as a diagnosis or a definitive statement about your health.

Why did my wellness score change when my routine stayed the same?

A routine is only one possible part of a wellness picture. A score or estimate may also depend on recorded check-ins, recent entries, available historical information, or changes to the data used by that feature.

Compare the underlying entries for the relevant period before concluding that the score is incorrect. Lumevine does not publish a verified, universal formula for every wellness score on this page, so it would be misleading to attribute a specific change to one particular input without further evidence.

Can missing sleep or mood entries affect my insights?

They can limit how much information is available for a summary or trend that depends on those records. Missing information should not automatically be treated as proof that something did or did not happen.

If you forgot to log an entry, add it if the app permits editing historical records. Otherwise, leave the gap as it is rather than entering an invented value.

Why does my mood-risk estimate change even when I feel the same?

An estimate is generated from app data and is not a direct measurement of how you feel. The records available to a calculation can change even when your subjective experience does not.

Use your own reflection as the primary account of your experience. Do not make a medical decision or conclude that you are at risk solely because an estimate changes.

The exact contribution of individual inputs should not be assumed unless it is explicitly documented by the feature.

Why isn't my newly added entry reflected in my score?

Saving an entry and updating a separate summary may not always happen at exactly the same moment. The summary may need to reload, or the relevant calculation may depend on additional information.

  1. Reopen the tracker and verify that the entry is present.
  2. Refresh the relevant dashboard or insight.
  3. Check that you are looking at the correct day or reporting period.
  4. If the entry is saved but the summary remains unchanged, report both the entry and the affected score.
Can I edit or correct a mood, sleep, or hydration entry?

Use the editing or removal controls available in the relevant tracker. If an edit control is not provided for a particular record, do not assume that changing a different screen will update it.

After making a change, reopen the tracker and verify the saved value. If the wrong value remains, contact support with the tracker name, the affected date, and what you tried.

What happens if I miss a day of tracking?

You can continue tracking on the next day. Depending on the feature, the missing day may appear as a gap or reduce the amount of information available for a trend.

A missed entry does not necessarily mean a score will become zero, and you should not invent past values merely to fill a gap.

How accurate are Lumevine's wellness scores and forecasts?

They are estimates or summaries based on the information and logic available to the relevant feature. Their usefulness depends on the accuracy and completeness of the recorded information and on the limitations of the calculation.

They are not clinical measurements, guaranteed predictions, or diagnoses. If a result conflicts with how you feel or with professional advice, do not treat the app's result as authoritative.

Does a low score mean I am doing something wrong?

No. A score is not a moral judgment or a complete description of your life. It may reflect limited data, a particular set of tracked habits, or the assumptions built into that feature.

Focus on useful observations and sustainable changes instead of trying to maximize a number at any cost.

Can I rely on a burnout forecast or recovery estimate?

Treat these features as prompts for reflection, not reliable predictions of a medical or psychological outcome. They cannot account for every personal, environmental, or health-related factor.

If you feel persistently exhausted, overwhelmed, or unwell, seek appropriate professional support regardless of the forecast.

How should I track something when I don't know the exact value?

Record an honest estimate only if the tracker supports estimates and you are comfortable providing one. If you do not know the value, leave it blank when possible. False precision can make later reflections less useful.

3. Routines, Habits, Tasks & Reminders

Help with schedules, activity durations, task lists, and reminders.

Why does my routine start at a different time than expected?

Check the routine's configured start time and the individual activity durations. Lumevine's routine scheduling can use the start time to position activities in the schedule, so changing that time may shift the displayed activity times.

If the schedule still differs from what you configured, compare the saved values with the displayed times and send support a description of the discrepancy.

What happens when I change my routine's start time?

Where the schedule is calculated relative to the routine start, changing that start can shift the activities together. It does not necessarily mean that each activity has been individually rescheduled.

Review the resulting schedule before relying on it. If you need a specific activity to remain at a fixed clock time, verify whether the relevant scheduling controls support that behavior.

Why is an activity missing from my routine?

Reopen the routine and check whether the activity is still configured, whether you edited a different routine, and whether the schedule reflects your latest changes.

If you recently changed the routine, refresh the page and inspect the saved configuration. If the activity remains missing, report its name and the steps that made it disappear.

Why does my routine show an unexpected duration or end time?

Check the configured durations and the order of the activities. If activities are arranged consecutively, changing an earlier duration may shift later activities and the overall end time.

Compare the total duration with the individual activity durations. If the displayed total does not match the saved configuration, contact support with a concrete example.

Can I create a routine that fits an irregular schedule?

Configure the activities and start times using the controls available in Lumevine. If your schedule changes frequently, treat the routine as a flexible guide rather than a commitment that must be followed exactly.

The exact support for recurring days, exceptions, and independent activity times depends on the controls available in your version of the routine editor.

Why is a completed task still showing as pending?

First verify that the completion action was accepted and that the correct task was updated. Refresh the task list and check whether you are viewing the intended day or list.

If the task still appears pending, avoid repeatedly creating replacement tasks. Report the task's visible status and the action you took so the problem can be investigated.

Why didn't my reminder notify me?

A reminder appearing in the app is not necessarily the same as a notification being delivered by your device. Delivery can depend on the reminder's implementation, browser or device permissions, operating-system settings, and whether the relevant app or browser supports that type of notification.

  1. Verify the reminder's saved date and time.
  2. Check browser or device notification permissions if applicable.
  3. Check whether the reminder type requires the app to remain open.
  4. Test with a new reminder and record the result.

Do not assume that all reminders are delivered as push notifications.

Will changing a routine erase my previous tracking history?

Editing a schedule and deleting historical records are different operations, but the exact effect depends on the feature's implementation. Before making a major change, use the available export or backup options if the history is important to you.

If a routine editor warns that existing records will be replaced or removed, read the warning carefully before continuing.

Why does my habit progress not match my actual effort?

Progress displays generally reflect recorded or completed items, not every effort you made outside the app. A missing completion entry can therefore differ from what actually happened in your day.

Check the relevant record and completion controls. Do not treat the displayed progress as a complete account of your effort.

4. AI Companion

Understand AI answers, conversation history, context, and limitations.

What can the Lumevine AI Companion actually do?

The AI Companion provides an AI-generated conversational experience. It can help you think through questions and reflect on information you provide in the conversation.

Its actual access to account records, trackers, previous conversations, or other app features depends on how the integration is implemented. Do not assume it can automatically see every item stored in Lumevine.

Can the AI Companion see my mood, sleep, and hydration logs?

The fact that these logs exist in Lumevine does not by itself establish that they are automatically sent to the AI Companion. Access depends on the information the app provides to the AI feature and the context of the conversation.

Until the exact integration and data flow are documented, avoid assuming either complete access or complete separation. Do not paste sensitive records into a chat unless you are comfortable sharing that information with the feature.

Why did the AI Companion give me a wrong or contradictory answer?

AI systems can misunderstand a question, overlook context, produce incorrect facts, or give different answers to similar questions. A fluent answer is not proof that the answer is correct.

  1. Restate the question and include the relevant context.
  2. Ask the Companion to distinguish known facts from assumptions.
  3. Verify important information independently.
  4. If the answer is repeatedly incorrect, report the prompt, the response, and the approximate time.

Remove passwords, private conversations, and other sensitive information before sharing examples with support.

Does the AI Companion remember my previous conversations?

Saved chat history and AI memory are not the same thing. A conversation may be stored for later viewing without every detail being automatically available in a new conversation.

Whether the Companion receives earlier messages, summaries, or persistent personal context depends on the integration. Do not rely on it remembering an important detail unless that behavior is explicitly confirmed by the feature.

Why does the AI Companion forget something I told it earlier?

The information may be outside the context available to the current response, the conversation may have changed, or the system may not support persistent memory for that detail.

Repeat the relevant information when needed. For anything important, keep your own reliable record rather than relying on an AI conversation to preserve it.

Is my AI Companion conversation history saved?

AI messages may be stored with your account to provide conversation history or related functionality. The exact retention period, deletion behavior, and access controls depend on the app's implementation and applicable privacy policy.

Review the Lumevine Privacy Policy for the applicable information. Avoid entering passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information into conversations.

What happens if I clear my AI chat history?

The effect depends on the chat controls provided by Lumevine. Removing visible messages may not necessarily remove every associated record, backup, or log held by the underlying services.

Read any confirmation shown by the app. If you need permanent deletion of associated personal data, contact support and ask specifically about chat history and related records.

Can I ask the AI Companion for a medical diagnosis?

You can ask general wellness questions, but the AI Companion is not a doctor, therapist, or diagnostic system. It can produce inaccurate or inappropriate suggestions and cannot replace a qualified professional's assessment.

Do not use an AI response to start, stop, or change medication or to make urgent health decisions.

Why is the AI Companion not responding or taking so long?

A slow or missing response can result from a network problem, a temporary service issue, a request failure, or a limit imposed by the underlying service.

  1. Check your connection and refresh the app.
  2. Check whether the message actually appeared in the conversation.
  3. Try a short, simple message to see whether the issue persists.
  4. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same long request while the original request may still be processing.
  5. Record any displayed error and contact support if it continues.
Why does the AI Companion give different answers to the same question?

AI-generated responses can vary with wording, conversation context, available information, and model behavior. A response is not necessarily a fixed result like a calculator output.

For questions requiring a consistent factual answer, check a reliable source and do not rely on the Companion as the only authority.

Can the AI Companion take actions in my account?

Do not assume that the Companion can create tasks, edit routines, change settings, or modify tracker records simply because it can discuss those activities. Such actions require explicit support in the app's AI integration.

Verify any proposed change in the actual feature and check whether the app confirms that the action was completed.

5. Reports, Trends & Reflections

Questions about missing reports, historical patterns, and summaries.

Why is my weekly report empty or incomplete?

A report can only summarize information available to the report feature. Missing check-ins, incomplete records, a period with little activity, or a loading problem can leave parts of a report empty or less informative.

  1. Check that you have selected the intended reporting period.
  2. Verify that relevant entries exist for those dates.
  3. Refresh the report and allow it to finish loading.
  4. If the report remains incomplete despite saved records, contact support with the period and missing section.
What dates does a weekly report cover?

Check the dates displayed by the report itself. Do not assume whether a week begins on Sunday or Monday, or whether it uses your local time zone, unless the feature explicitly indicates so.

If the displayed period appears incorrect, include the dates shown and your expected period when contacting support.

Why is a pattern or trend missing from my insights?

A trend may require more records or a longer observation period than you currently have. Gaps, inconsistent entries, or a feature's particular requirements can also affect what is shown.

Continue recording relevant information consistently when practical. Do not assume that the absence of a displayed trend proves there is no relationship or pattern.

Can I compare my wellness data across different weeks?

Use the historical views or reporting controls available in Lumevine. For a meaningful comparison, make sure the periods being compared are clearly identified and that differences in missing data are taken into account.

A difference between two summaries does not by itself establish what caused the change.

Why does a report disagree with what I remember?

The report reflects the information available to it, which may not capture everything that happened. Review the underlying entries for the relevant period and check for omissions or incorrectly recorded values.

If the underlying records are correct but the report still appears inconsistent, send support a specific example rather than relying on the summary alone.

Can I export a report or share it with someone?

Use the export or sharing controls that are actually available for the report you want to use. An option to export general app data does not necessarily mean that every report has its own downloadable format.

Before sharing, review the contents for personal wellness information that you do not want others to receive.

Why does my recovery outlook not match how recovered I feel?

A recovery outlook is an estimate, not a direct measurement of your physical or mental state. It may not account for all factors affecting your experience.

Give appropriate weight to how you feel and to professional advice. Do not push through pain, severe exhaustion, or other concerning symptoms just because an estimate suggests you are ready.

What can my friends see about my Lumevine activity?

Visibility depends on the information the app makes available through its friend and sharing features. Do not assume that every tracker is private by default or that every tracker is automatically shared.

Review any sharing controls and the information shown in the relevant social screen before connecting with someone. The exact visibility of each data field should be confirmed by the app's actual access controls and Privacy Policy.

What happens when I accept a friend request?

Accepting a request establishes a connection within the app. It should not be assumed to grant access to every item in your account, but the information made available through that connection depends on the app's sharing implementation.

Check the resulting friend profile and available privacy controls if you want to understand what has become visible.

Why can't I find a friend or see their profile?

Verify that you are searching for the correct account and that the connection feature has loaded. The other person may not be discoverable through the method you are using, or a request may not have been completed.

Refresh the screen and check the status of any existing request. If the problem persists, contact support without sharing private information about the other person's account.

What if I sent a friend request to the wrong person?

Look for an available cancel, remove, or disconnect control in the relevant social screen. If no such control is available, contact support for guidance.

Can I disconnect from a friend later?

Use the connection management controls provided by the app, if available. Removing a connection may change future visibility, but it does not necessarily delete information the other person has already seen or independently recorded.

Does sharing wellness information mean my data is public?

Not necessarily. Sharing through a friend feature and publishing information publicly are different things. However, the exact audience depends on the controls provided by Lumevine.

7. Accounts, Privacy & Data Management

Understand cloud storage, browser storage, exports, imports, and deletion.

Where does Lumevine store my information?

Lumevine uses Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage, and the app may use browser storage for preferences or session state.

The precise storage location and retention behavior can differ by data type and service. Read the Privacy Policy for the applicable details.

Does Lumevine sell my personal information?

According to the Lumevine Privacy Policy, Lumevine does not sell personal information. Information may still be processed by service providers needed to operate the app.

For details about applicable providers and data handling, consult the Privacy Policy .

What is the difference between browser storage and cloud data?

Browser storage is information kept by the browser on your device. Cloud data is stored by a remote service associated with the app. Different kinds of information can be stored in different places.

Clearing browser data may remove local preferences, session information, or other locally stored records. It should not be assumed to delete all cloud records, and deleting a cloud record does not necessarily clear every local copy.

Will clearing my browser data delete my Lumevine account?

Clearing browser data is not the same as explicitly deleting your account. It can remove local session information and may require you to sign in again.

Before clearing storage, ensure that you know how to regain access to your account. Do not rely on a browser session as the only way to access important information.

What does “Clear All Data” actually delete?

Use the confirmation message displayed by the app as the primary reference for determining the scope of the operation. Clearing app data and deleting an account are two distinct actions. Clearing app data removes all account-related data stored locally within the app but does not delete the account or its associated credentials.

  1. Read the confirmation carefully before proceeding.
  2. Export any information you want to preserve, if export is available.
  3. Check whether the operation refers to local data, cloud data, or both.
  4. Afterward, verify which information remains and whether you can still sign in.

If the scope is unclear, contact support before confirming a potentially destructive operation.

Why is my data still visible after I cleared app data?

The operation may not cover every storage location or account record. Information might remain in cloud storage, browser storage, another signed-in device, or a separate feature's records.

Review what the clear-data action says it affects. If the data should have been removed but remains visible, record where it appears and contact support rather than repeatedly clearing unrelated storage.

How do I export my Lumevine data?

Use the data export control available in Lumevine, if present, and follow the instructions shown by the app. Check the resulting file and its contents before relying on it as a complete backup.

Export formats and coverage can differ. Do not assume that a single export contains every chat, preference, account record, or server-side log unless the export description explicitly says so.

Does exporting my data delete it from Lumevine?

Exporting and deleting are separate operations. Creating a downloadable copy should not be treated as a request to remove the original data from your account.

If you want deletion, use the relevant deletion controls or contact support separately.

What happens when I import data from another account?

Lumevine's import workflow describes replacing current data with the source account's latest cloud backup. Treat this as a potentially destructive operation, not a simple merge.

  1. Verify that the source account is the intended account.
  2. Export your current data first, if the export option is available.
  3. Read all warnings and confirmation messages.
  4. Proceed only if you understand that existing data may be replaced.
  5. After the import, check important records and account state.

The import workflow also indicates that the source account receives a security warning on its next login. If the warning or replacement behavior differs from what you see, contact support.

Will importing data merge both accounts together?

Do not assume that import means merge. Lumevine's described workflow replaces current data with a source backup, so you should assume that destination records may be overwritten.

If you need to combine information from two accounts, ask support whether a supported merge process exists before performing an import.

How do I permanently delete my Lumevine account?

Email support.lumevine@gmail.com to request account deletion and the removal of associated personal data.

Clearly state that you want account deletion, not merely a local data reset. Support may need to verify account ownership. Ask for clarification if you need to understand which records can be deleted and whether any retention obligations apply.

Does deleting my account also delete my AI chats and backups?

Do not assume that every related record is removed immediately by a single action. Chat history, backups, service logs, and other records can have different retention and deletion behavior.

Include AI chat history and backups in your deletion request if you want support to clarify their treatment. Refer to the Privacy Policy for the applicable retention terms.

Can Lumevine access information on my device?

Access depends on the permissions and capabilities used by the features you interact with. Browser storage, device permissions, and information you submit are different kinds of access.

Review your browser or device permissions and the Privacy Policy if you want to understand how particular information is handled.

How can I request a copy of my personal information?

Contact support.lumevine@gmail.com and describe the information you want to access. If the app's export function meets your needs, you can also use that control.

Support may need to verify your account before handling a request. Do not send your password or authentication codes by email.

8. Technical Support & Troubleshooting

Practical steps for loading problems, saving failures, and unexpected behavior.

Lumevine is stuck loading. What should I do?
  1. Wait briefly to see whether the page finishes loading.
  2. Check that your internet connection is working.
  3. Refresh the page or reopen the app.
  4. Try another supported browser if the problem continues.
  5. If an error appears, note its exact wording.

Avoid clearing all browser storage as your first troubleshooting step. Doing so can remove local state and sign-in information without necessarily fixing the underlying problem.

Why are my changes not being saved?

A visible change on the screen does not always mean it was successfully saved. A network failure, a rejected request, or a temporary application issue may prevent the change from being persisted.

  1. Look for a save confirmation or error message.
  2. Reopen the relevant section and check the saved value.
  3. Check your connection and retry once if appropriate.
  4. Do not close the page until an important change is confirmed.
  5. If it fails again, report the action and the resulting error.
My data disappeared after I signed in again. Has it been deleted?

Not necessarily. First check that you signed in using the intended account and that you are viewing the correct section or reporting period. Local-only information and cloud-saved information may behave differently.

Avoid creating large amounts of replacement data or importing another backup until you understand what happened. If important records are missing, contact support and describe when they were last visible.

Why isn't my data syncing between devices?

First verify that both devices are using the same intended account. Data stored locally may not automatically appear on another device unless the relevant feature saves it to the cloud.

  1. Check the account on each device.
  2. Confirm that the record appears on the device where it was created.
  3. Refresh the destination device and revisit the relevant feature.
  4. Check for an error or an indication that saving failed.
  5. Contact support if the records remain inconsistent.
What should I do if I lose access to my account?

Use the sign-in or recovery options provided by Lumevine. Make sure you are using the correct authentication method and account details.

If you cannot recover access, email support.lumevine@gmail.com with a description of the issue. Never send your password, one-time authentication code, or recovery secret.

Why does Lumevine look broken on my phone or browser?

Display issues can result from an outdated browser, zoom or viewport settings, blocked scripts, cached resources, or a temporary loading failure.

  1. Refresh the page.
  2. Return browser zoom to a normal setting.
  3. Update your browser if an update is available.
  4. Try a private window or another supported browser.
  5. Report the affected screen and device/browser details.
Should I clear my cache or all site data to fix an error?

A normal refresh is the safer first step. Clearing cached files can sometimes resolve a stale-resource problem, but clearing all site data may also remove session information and locally stored records.

Before clearing site data, make sure you know how to sign in again and have exported any important information that might exist only locally.

Why is a button not responding?

The control may be waiting for data, disabled until a required field is completed, or affected by a temporary script or network error.

Check whether any required fields are missing, wait for any loading indicator, and try refreshing. If the issue persists, include the button's label, the screen where it appears, and what happens when you select it.

What information should I include in a bug report?

A useful report helps support reproduce the issue without requiring access to your private account information.

  1. The feature or page where the issue occurred.
  2. The steps you took immediately beforehand.
  3. What you expected to happen.
  4. What actually happened, including any exact error message.
  5. Your browser or device type and the approximate time.
  6. A screenshot with private information removed, if helpful.

Never include your password, authentication codes, payment information, or unnecessary personal wellness records.

What if the problem happens only sometimes?

Intermittent problems are easier to investigate when you can describe when they occur. Note the affected feature, approximate time, steps, and whether refreshing changes the result.

If a problem happens repeatedly, send support those observations even if you cannot reproduce it every time.

What if the app displays an error I don't understand?

Record the exact error text and the action that triggered it. Avoid guessing what the message means or repeating an action that could overwrite or duplicate important information.

Send the error to support.lumevine@gmail.com with a short description of what you were trying to do.
