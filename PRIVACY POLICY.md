# Lumevine Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 15, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumevine handles information when you use the Lumevine application. We use information to operate the app, sync your account, and provide its features. Please avoid entering information you would not want stored with your account.

## 1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Lumevine may store:

- Account details such as your email address, username, display name, and account-related timestamps.
- Information you enter into the app, such as tasks, reminders, routine activities, mood logs, water and sleep logs, bedtime settings, gratitude notes, and challenge or rescue activity.
- Messages and responses in your AI Companion chat history.
- Friend usernames, requests, connections, and information needed to provide friend-related features.
- Data you choose to include in an account export or import.

## 2. Information stored in your browser

Lumevine uses browser storage, including local storage and session storage, for app preferences and features such as theme selection, selected currency, and short-lived session state. You can clear browser data through your browser settings; doing so may remove saved preferences or sign you out.

## 3. How information is used

Information is used to:

- Create and maintain your account and authenticate sign-ins.
- Save and display your logs, tasks, reminders, routine, and other app data across sessions.
- Generate dashboard summaries, trends, wellness scores, and assistant responses based on information available in the app.
- Enable friend requests and the friend features you choose to use.
- Provide data export/import, enforce usage limits, maintain reliability, and protect accounts and the service.

Wellness scores, mood-risk estimates, burnout forecasts, trends, and suggested recovery actions are estimates based on information available in the app. They may be incomplete, inaccurate, or out of date. They are for general informational and self-reflection purposes only—not medical assessments, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations, and not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use them to make medical decisions. If you have health concerns, seek advice from a qualified professional.

## 4. AI Companion

AI Companion messages may be stored with your account so the app can display chat history and provide conversation features. Do not use the AI Companion to submit passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information. The AI Companion is not a medical professional and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

## 5. When information is shared

Lumevine does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to run the app, including Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Friend-related information is used to provide the friend features you choose to use.

The app includes an embedded Spotify player. If you use it, Spotify may receive information about your interaction with its player under Spotify’s Privacy Policy: https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/. The app is hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information associated with visiting the site under GitHub’s Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect users, accounts, and the service.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained while needed to provide the app and its features, meet operational requirements, and address security or legal obligations. You can use the app’s data controls, including Clear All Data and data export, where available. Clearing app data does not necessarily delete your sign-in account. To request account deletion or ask about data associated with your account, contact us using the address below. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is required by law.

## 7. Security

We use service-provider security features and reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Children

Lumevine is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the request.

## 9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can review or update certain account details and use the app’s data controls. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your account before responding.

## 10. International processing

Lumevine’s service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Privacy protections may differ between locations.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published. Continued use of Lumevine after an update means the revised policy applies to future use.

## 12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support.lumevine@gmail.com
