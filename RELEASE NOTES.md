# Release Notes:

- Help and info: FAQ links now cover updated app behavior; About Us is available from the FAQ. Support and legal links open in a browser tab.

- AI memory and conversations: Clearing AI chats keeps saved memory; Clear All Data removes it. AI follow-ups are more context-aware, including short replies to check-ins and other questions.

- Faster feedback: Water, sleep, mood, gratitude, tasks, water goals, and bedtime reminders update the UI immediately while saving. If a save fails, the app restores the previous state and reports the error.

- Sign-in polish: Canceling Google sign-in clears its progress message. Optional Firestore permission errors during Google sign-up no longer produce misleading error popups; actual write failures still surface.

- Patterns & Progress: Task totals are labeled as today’s tasks, and the summary bar changes with the selected metric—tasks, sleep, water, or mood.

- Loading: A longer-than-usual dashboard load now shows a helpful message after seven seconds.

- Habit Journey: The streak and weekly tracker were refined for clearer dates, better contrast, and a calmer feel. The weekly tracker fits without horizontal scrolling; progress and optional bonus styling remain.

- Welcome Guide: Updated with current guidance on check-ins, routines, AI memory, patterns, friends, and account controls. Returning users who completed the older guide will see the update once; it’s also available under Account → Help. Links are easier to read.

- Search descriptions: Added custom meta descriptions for the main app, FAQ, About Us, and Privacy Policy.
